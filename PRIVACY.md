# Notice Window privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Notice Window is a Forge app that runs on Atlassian's platform inside your Confluence and Jira site.

## What the app reads
As the person using it and with that person's permissions: the pages of the Confluence space they open the Notice
Window page in (title, body and link), the page they are viewing (to show its notice day in the byline), and, when they
create tasks, the Jira project they name and existing tasks that carry the app's labels.

## What the app writes
Only when a person asks for it: fictional sample pages (Load sample contracts) and Jira tasks with a link to the
contract page (Create tasks). Everything it writes stays in your Atlassian site.

## What the app stores
Nothing. The app has no database or storage of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian. The developer does not
collect, receive or sell personal data. Atlassian's platform keeps its usual operational logs for Forge apps; see
Atlassian's privacy policy.

## Contact
Questions and requests: the issues page of the app's public code repository.
